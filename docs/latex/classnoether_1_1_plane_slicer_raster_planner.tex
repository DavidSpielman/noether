An implementation of the Raster Planner using a series of parallel cutting planes. This implementation works best on approximately planar parts. The direction generator defines the direction of the raster cut. The cut normal (i.\+e., the raster step direction) is defined by the cross product of the cut direction and the smallest principal axis of the mesh.
